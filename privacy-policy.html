Privacy Policy

Effective date: January 1, 2026 · Last updated: November 2, 2026

Nearby Calls, a service of Sportsman Creative ("Nearby Calls," "we," "us"), based in Springfield, MO, provides Google Business Profile optimization, reputation management, and review generation for local businesses. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

  • Business information, such as your Google Business Profile data, reviews, photos, and performance metrics.
  • Technical data, such as IP address, device and browser details, and cookies when you use our website.

2. Your Customers' Data

To send review requests on your behalf, you may provide customer contact information (such as names, phone numbers, and email addresses). You are responsible for having the necessary consent to share it. We use this data only to deliver the services you requested, we do not claim ownership of it, and we do not use it for our own marketing.

3. How We Use Information

  • Providing and supporting the services, including optimizing profiles and managing review campaigns
  • Processing payments

  • Improving our services and website
  • Meeting legal obligations

4. Text Messages (SMS)

You consent to receive text messages from us by opting in (for example, by providing your mobile number for service communications or requesting a texted example). Message frequency varies and standard message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for assistance. SMS opt-in consent is not shared with third parties for marketing purposes. We do not sell personal information to third parties.

5. Email

You may receive service and account emails, and marketing emails if you have requested them. You can unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time using the link in the email.

6. Review Requests

We may send review and feedback requests to your customers on your behalf. You must obtain your customers' consent before providing their contact information for this purpose.

7. AI-Assisted Services

We use AI tools to support profile optimization, content creation, review responses, and reporting insights. AI outputs may occasionally be inaccurate or outdated; we review our work, and clients should tell us about anything that needs correction. We do not guarantee the accuracy of AI-generated outputs.

8. Service Providers

We work with third-party providers — such as CRM, hosting, payment processing, analytics, advertising, and AI providers — who process data on our behalf under appropriate safeguards. Each provider has its own privacy practices.

9. Sharing

We share information only with the service providers above, with payment processors to complete transactions, with legal authorities when required by law, or with a successor in the event of a merger or acquisition. We do not sell personal information.

10. Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

11. Cookies

Our website uses cookies for traffic analysis, user experience, and advertising. You can manage cookies through your browser settings.

12. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information, object to certain processing, or withdraw consent. Send requests to team@nearbycalls.com and we will respond as required by applicable law.
